HOW TO PLAY BACKTRACK

BACKTRACK started out as a 70’s/80’s music nostalgia game, but due to the interest of different age groups, it’s expanded to include music from the 90’s and 2000’s and some oldies, show tunes, and jazz standards. It’s a great little game to play at parties, on long car trips, or any other time you’re in the mood to revisit some old musical favorites!

The premise is simple: the clue giver does their best to produce the melody by humming, whistling, scat singing, or whatever other techniques they might want to use. The guessers have 45 seconds (or you can be more generous) to name the song, and they earn bonus points for artist and year. It’s a good idea to allow a certain amount of latitude when guessing the year, especially for the older songs. We’ll let you decide. For the special categories, those bonus points might instead be for naming the Broadway show or the decade the song first became popular. Each team gets a certain number of passes depending on the length of the game.

BACKTRACK can be played competitively with up to 4 teams, or you can just select “Party Mode” and try for a high score. To accommodate different ages and skill levels, each team gets to design its own “deck” by choosing from different eras, difficulties, and genres.

However you choose to play, I hope you enjoy taking a trip down a musical memory lane!

Paul Titus, game designer
